## Filter false negatives and exact-state protocol

Canonical results: `output/recoverability_filter_coarse54_20261112_v1/`.

Every branch copies MuJoCo `mjSTATE_INTEGRATION` and restores the identical physical model, motor target, original front-foot anchors and accumulated safety history. Bank continuation also restores policy previous action, CPG phase and filtered loads. Perturbations are generated by 40 ms of bounded joint motor commands followed by forward integration, never by editing pose, velocity, contacts or contact forces. Policy continuation retains the resulting controller observations/memory. Scratch worlds do not alter live states. Tests check position, velocity, contacts, acceleration warm-start and observations.

The empirical envelope rejected all 12 near-boundary perturbation tests. **6/12 still recovered** with unchanged five-second hold, collision, joint, speed, torque, support, drift and slip-speed criteria. Because the bank has duplicate states, these are only **4 unique perturbation cases, of which 2 recover**; no population false-negative-rate estimate is justified. The negative thigh-offset cases failed the original 0.001 rad joint-limit tolerance around 3.46 s; these failures were retained. Positive cases passed. Exact-state tests at all 20 kick closest approaches failed, so removing the envelope alone does not solve the kick-to-balance gap.

The filter is a computational proposal heuristic, not a necessary physical condition for recoverability. Keep exact-state recovery validation mandatory and bypass the heuristic for controlled diagnostics. Do not broaden live safety acceptance based on these tests.

## Transition feasibility and leg motions

Canonical physics-rate results: `output/recoverability_transition_physics_coarse54_20261112_v1/`. Search starts at the closest earlier kick states with no prior physical failure, front support, drift ≤0.04 m and cumulative loaded slip ≤0.02 m. It uses 0.4 s trajectories, three motor-target knots and paired front/rear hip/thigh/calf offsets bounded by ±0.5 rad. All native motors, limits, collisions, gravity and contacts remain active. There are 3 CEM iterations × 12 candidates × 3 starts = **108 candidate trajectories**. Frozen-policy five-second recovery dominates the score; distance, slip and drift are secondary. Pitch alone is never the objective. Candidate recovery is a deterministic empirical indicator, not a calibrated probability estimate; no held-out transition probability can be claimed without successful references.

No candidate recovered. Best references are saved for inspection, not admitted as successful demonstrations. Search feasibility is a finite-budget result, not proof that a physical transition is impossible.

## Reverse curriculum and morphology

`experiments/recoverability/reverse_curriculum.py` implements validated snapshot admission, morphology-separated sampling, parent-linked reverse expansion through validated motor trajectories, and advancement gates. `validate_transition.py` produces a serialized seed bank and evaluates paired physical morphology proposals. No interpolation of physical state between models is permitted.

Start with independently validated balance states. Admit an earlier integrated state only after its motor trajectory and the frozen-policy continuation pass. Expand the reference horizon by 40 ms from 40 ms toward 0.8 s only after at least 20 held-out original-Go2 evaluations, recovery rate ≥80%, Wilson lower bound ≥0.60, no collisions or motor/joint violations, loaded slip ≤0.02 m and drift ≤0.04 m. Every successful recovery must hold five seconds. Pilot criteria additionally require multiple start seeds and strictly separated evaluation seeds. Final evaluation must start standing on morphology 1.00 and validate the complete sequence.

The morphology probe uses the existing physical leg-scaling assets at 1.00, 0.95, 0.90, 0.85 and 0.80, with fresh per-model IK proposals at 90, 88, 85, 80, 70, 60 and 35 degrees. All 35 are feasible instantaneous proposals; only **1/35**, nominal 90 degrees at scale 1.00, recovers. Only that state is admitted to the current reverse seed bank. Scaled morphologies are out of the frozen balance policy's training distribution; these failures do not prove shorter legs cannot help a separately trained transition controller. Raw COM position, loaded slip/drift, contact forces, collision and motor outcomes are saved per proposal. Learning difficulty was not measured because training remains gated. There is no evidence here to justify selecting a shortened morphology.

No feasible transition was identified, so the conditional two-update smoke and separate ten-update PPO pilot were **not enabled or launched**. The present curriculum is a validated-state bank and expansion/gating implementation; it has not learned an expansion toward standing.
